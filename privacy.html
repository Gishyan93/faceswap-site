Doppel Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026.

Doppel is made by Tigran Gishyan. This policy explains what happens to the photos, videos and faces you use in the app. The short version: everything stays on your device.

Your photos and videos

Face swaps are made on your device. The photos and videos you pick, and the swaps the app makes from them, are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Doppel has no servers and no accounts.

You choose photos and videos with the system picker, which gives the app only the items you pick, not your whole library.

Saved faces

When you save a face to reuse it, the app keeps a small picture of that face and a face description: a list of numbers the app uses to recognise the same face again. Both stay on your device and are used only to make your swaps. They are never shared, never used for advertising, and never used to identify you.

Saved swaps

Swaps you keep are stored in the app on your device.

Hidden swaps

Swaps you hide are encrypted on your device. The key that opens them is kept in this device's Keychain only: it is not synced to iCloud and does not move to a new device through a backup. This means hidden swaps can't be opened on another device.

The Hidden folder opens with Face ID or your passcode. iOS does that check; the app never sees your face scan or your passcode.

Deleting your data

Deleting a swap or a saved face in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored, except two small items in your device's Keychain, which iOS keeps after an app is deleted: a marker that your free swap has been used (so it stays used if the app is reinstalled), and the key for hidden swaps, which opens nothing once the swaps themselves are gone. Neither contains anything about you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, your Apple ID or your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it shares no data with third parties. The only thing it fetches from the internet is this page.

Permissions

The app asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Photos (add only): to save a swap to your photo library. The app can add to your library but can't read it.
  • Face ID: to open the Hidden folder.

You can change these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

Doppel is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from anyone. Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing we hold about any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
